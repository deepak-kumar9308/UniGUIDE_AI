Viva Explanation of Metrics
  • Accuracy: How often predictions were correct across all classes.
  • Precision: Out of all predicted positive outcomes, how many were true positives.
  • Recall: Out of all actual positive outcomes, how many were successfully identified.
  • F1-Score: Harmonic mean balancing precision and recall.
