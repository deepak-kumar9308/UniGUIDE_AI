# Project Overview — UniGuide AI

UniGuide AI is a realistic, AI-powered EdTech college admission counseling platform designed to guide students from account registration to college selection, admission prediction, scholarship discovery, and application tracking.

## Core Problem Addressed
High school and undergraduate students face complex decision-making during college admissions:
- Information fragmentation across college portals.
- Lack of explainable admission probability estimates.
- Difficulty finding relevant merit scholarships before application deadlines.
- Disorganized document tracking for counseling sessions.

## Target Student Flow
`Student Registration` -> `Profile Completion` -> `College & Course Exploration` -> `AI College Recommendation` -> `Admission Chance Prediction` -> `Scholarship Discovery` -> `Side-by-Side College Comparison` -> `Document Guidance` -> `24/7 AI Chatbot` -> `Personalized Student & Admin Dashboard`

## Key Capabilities
- **6-Factor Weighted Recommendation Engine**: Academic compatibility (30%), Course match (25%), Budget (15%), Location (10%), Career alignment (10%), Admission compatibility (10%).
- **Predictive Admission Probability**: ML model predicting HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW chances with probability gauge and actionable improvement tips.
- **Scholarship Discovery & Matching**: Active vs EXPIRED scholarship classification based on deadlines and student merit.
- **Document Guidance Module**: Status tracking for 8 key admission documents + AI Document Counselor with institutional disclaimers.
- **Admin Dashboard**: System tallies, student management, college CRUD, scholarship CRUD, Chart.js analytics, and ML model confusion matrix evaluation metrics.
